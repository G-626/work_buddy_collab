# Social Story — Marco L. (FICTIONAL DEMO DATA) (2026-08-11)

The bakery is a big room. Many people work there.

When I am not sure what to do next, I can ask: "What should I do next?"

Many people take a pause before the next step. Taking a pause is fine —
then I ask, or a grown-up shows me the first step.

When the machine is loud, I can put on my sound support before my ears
need it. That works well.

At the end of the morning, someone says: "Before we tidy up: 5 more
minutes, then we tidy the trays together."

*This is a story about a real situation, written with the student for practice at
home and at school. Fictional demo data.*
