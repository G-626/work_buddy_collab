# Social Story — Priya S. (FICTIONAL DEMO DATA) (2026-08-10)

The art room has one big table. Everyone has a job.

When the class is not sure what to do next, they can ask: "What should I do next?"

Many people pause before a new step. Taking a pause is fine — then they
ask, or the teacher shows the first step.

At the end of the lesson, the teacher says: "5 more minutes, then we
tidy the tables together."

*This is a story about a real situation, written with the student for practice at
home and at school. Fictional demo data.*
