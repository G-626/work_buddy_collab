## 1. Teacher Guide

**Student:** Marco L. | **Date:** 2025-08-05 | **Situation:** First work-experience placement at Sunbeam Bakery, Tuesday 19 August

**What to expect**
- Marco will arrive on time and follow instructions precisely.
- He may ask very detailed questions about rules and routines.
- He may not ask for help if unsure — he will struggle silently instead.
- Loud machine noise (mixers) may cause him to cover his ears or freeze.
- He will benefit from knowing the plan in advance.

**What helps**
- Give him a written step list for each task before he starts.
- Remind him he has ear defenders in his bag when mixers are running.
- Tell him explicitly: "It is okay to ask questions."
- Give him a 5-minute warning before any transition (e.g. break time, end of shift).
- If he seems stuck, ask directly: "Do you know what to do next?"

**What to avoid**
- Waiting for him to ask for help — he may not.
- Vague instructions like "help out" or "pitch in."
- Surprising him with changes to the plan.

**Language to use**
- "You can say, 'Can I take a five-minute break?'"
- "You can say, 'What should I do next?'"
- "It is okay to ask questions."

**Warning signs**
- Covering ears, going still or silent → reduce noise if possible, offer a short break.
- Repeatedly checking the time or the plan → reassure him the schedule has not changed.
