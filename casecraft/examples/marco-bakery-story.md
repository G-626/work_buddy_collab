## 1. Draft story

**My First Day at Sunbeam Bakery**

On Tuesday 19 August, I will start a work-experience placement at Sunbeam Bakery.
I will work from 9:00 in the morning until 3:30 in the afternoon.
Sunbeam Bakery is near Mong Kok MTR station, Exit B2.

I will travel from Jordan station to Mong Kok station on the Tsuen Wan Line.
That is 3 stops: Jordan, Yau Ma Tei, Mong Kok.
Exit B2 is the closest exit to the bakery.

The supervisor at the bakery is Mrs. Chan.
A supervisor is the person who tells workers what to do and answers their questions.
Mrs. Chan knows that this is my first day.

My jobs will be bagging rolls and labelling boxes.
Mrs. Chan will show me how to do each job.
Most people do not know how to do the jobs on their first day.
Learning the jobs is the point of work experience.

The kitchen is warm, and the mixers are loud.
Loud machines can be uncomfortable.
I have ear defenders in my school bag. I can put them on when the mixers are running.
If I need a short break, I can say to Mrs. Chan, "Can I take a five-minute break?"
Taking a short break is okay.

When I am not sure what to do, I can ask Mrs. Chan.
I can say, "What should I do next?"
Asking questions is what workers do when something is new.

At 3:30, my work day will finish. I will travel home from Mong Kok station.
My first day will be complete.

When I feel unsure, I can ______. *(complete together)*
