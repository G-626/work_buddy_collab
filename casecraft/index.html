The Problem

Social workers and therapists supporting adolescents with autism spend most of their time on paperwork, not face-to-face support. When a student faces a new situation — a class trip, a work placement, a change of routine — the professional must prepare separate materials for the teacher, the parent, and sometimes the student themselves.

Each document takes 30–60 minutes to write well. And when they're written separately, they drift: the teacher says one thing, the parent says another, and the student gets mixed messages at exactly the moment they need consistency most.

What CaseCraft Does

CaseCraft is a custom agent skill for Tencent WorkBuddy. A social worker types one command with a situation brief. CaseCraft reads the student's curated dossier and generates a Coordinated Support Pack — a set of audience-specific documents that agree with each other because they're generated from the same source and checked by the same linter.

1

One command

/casecraft marco "First work-experience placement at Sunbeam Bakery next Tuesday, 9:00–15:30. Kitchen is warm and mixers are loud."

2

Dossier-driven personalisation

The student's profile — reading level, triggers, coping strategies, special interests — selects what each audience needs to know. No re-pasting, no re-explaining.

3

Coordinated Support Pack generated

Teacher Guide (classroom strategies, warning signs), Parent Guide (home reinforcement, consistent language), and optionally a Social Story (teen-facing, methodology-compliant).

4

Cross-document consistency check

The linter verifies that coping scripts, transition cues, and factual claims are identical across all documents. Contradictions are flagged for professional review.

Why Not Just Use a Chatbot?

A professional can paste notes into Gemini and get a plausible document. But a chatbot structurally cannot do what CaseCraft does:

Generic Chatbot

  • No audit against evidence-based methodology
  • No memory of the student's history or past materials
  • Re-paste the dossier for every new document
  • Documents drift — no cross-document consistency
  • No local record or filing system

CaseCraft

  • Linter checks every output; visible pass/fix/flag report
  • Longitudinal dossier keeps language consistent over months
  • One brief → multiple coordinated documents
  • Cross-document consistency check catches contradictions
  • Files saved per student, indexed and searchable

The Demo Moment

The 5-minute demo shows two fictional students — Marco (14, literal thinker, MTR enthusiast, noise-sensitive) and Priya (15, socially anxious, visual learner, art interest) — receiving visibly different Coordinated Support Packs for the same situation.

Lead: The Identical Sentence

The judge sees one coping script appear word-for-word in both the Teacher Guide and the Parent Guide:

"I can say, 'I need a short break.'"

The teacher and the parent are telling the student the exact same thing, in the exact same words. That doesn't happen today.

Follow: The Contradiction Catch

A deliberate contradiction planted in the fictional dossier (5-min vs 10-min transition warning) is flagged by the linter:

FLAG: Teacher Guide says 5-min warning; Parent Guide says 10-min warning → verify with venue

The tool catches a mistake a human would miss.
