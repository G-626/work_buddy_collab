The Problem

An autistic student in Hong Kong is supported by a constellation of adults: a classroom teacher, a parent, a therapist, a school social worker. Each rebuilds an understanding of the same child from scratch — through meetings, forwarded notes, and guesswork. Information lives in fragmented silos, goes stale, and never reaches the right person in the right form at the right time.

Every school transition, new teacher, or new situation resets this understanding to zero. Social workers spend ~20% of their time face-to-face with clients — the rest is paperwork.

What CaseCraft Does

CaseCraft is a custom agent skill for Tencent WorkBuddy. Its core artifact is the Student Passport — a living, versioned, single-source-of-truth document per student that coordinates every adult around the child.

The passport is fed by a session agent loop: the worker sets a goal, observes the session, reviews a structured draft, and approves it. The passport updates. Stakeholder views — Teacher Guide, Parent Guide, Social Story — re-render from the updated passport, all linter-checked.

1

Set the session goal

/session marco --goal="ask for help when unsure, no more than 2 prompts"

2

Capture and ingest

The capture layer produces observables only — events, quotes, counts, timestamps. No emotional inference. The ingestion skill drafts a structured session summary.

3

Review gate

The worker approves or edits the draft in ~90 seconds. Nothing merges into the passport without human approval.

4

Passport delta + views

The approved session merges into the passport. All stakeholder views regenerate from the updated passport, linted for methodology, consistency, and sensitivity leaks.

Why Not Just Use a Chatbot?

A professional can paste session notes into Gemini and get a plausible teacher guide. But a chatbot structurally cannot do what CaseCraft does:

Generic Chatbot

  • No audit against evidence-based methodology
  • No memory of the student's history or past materials
  • Re-paste the dossier for every new document
  • Documents drift — no cross-document consistency
  • No sensitivity controls — clinical info can leak into parent views

CaseCraft

  • Linter checks every output; visible pass/fix/flag report
  • Living passport accumulates across sessions and months
  • One session → all views regenerate from one source
  • Cross-document consistency + sensitivity leak checks
  • Files saved per student, versioned, freshness-flagged

The Demo Moment

The 5-minute demo shows the full agent loop for two fictional students — Marco (14, literal thinker, MTR enthusiast, noise-sensitive) and Priya (15, socially anxious, visual learner, art interest).

Lead: The Identical Sentence

The judge sees one coping script appear word-for-word in both the Teacher Guide and the Parent Guide:

"I can say, 'I need a short break.'"

The teacher and the parent are telling the student the exact same thing, in the exact same words. That doesn't happen today.

Follow: The Sensitivity Leak Catch

A planted leak — clinical information appearing in a Parent Guide — is caught by the linter:

FAIL: Clinical-tagged section appeared in Parent Guide → blocked from delivery

The system catches its own problems, file-verified. No chatbot does this.

Close: The Freshness Flag

A passport section untouched for >30 days surfaces a flag:

FLAG: "Special interests" last updated 32 days ago — consider reviewing

A stale passport is a liability. Perceiving staleness keeps the passport trustworthy.
