## Transcript (simulated)

**09:05** — Marco arrives at Sunbeam Bakery, 5 minutes early. He is wearing his ear
defenders around his neck. Mrs. Chan greets him and shows him to the kitchen.

**09:08** — Mrs. Chan demonstrates bagging rolls. Marco watches, nods, does not ask
questions. He begins bagging rolls independently.

**09:15** — Mixer starts. Marco covers his ears with both hands, stops working.
Mrs. Chan points to his ear defenders. Marco puts them on, resumes work.

**09:22** — Marco finishes bagging the first tray. He stands still, looks at the tray,
looks at Mrs. Chan, looks at the tray again. He does not speak. 45 seconds pass.

**09:23** — K. Wong (observing) prompts: "Marco, what can you say?" Marco turns to
Mrs. Chan and says: "What should I do next?" Mrs. Chan shows him the labelling station.

**09:30** — Marco is labelling boxes. He picks up a label, looks at it, puts it down,
picks up another. He does not speak. 30 seconds pass.

**09:31** — K. Wong prompts: "Marco, what can you say?" Marco says to Mrs. Chan:
"Where does this label go?" Mrs. Chan shows him. Marco continues labelling.

**09:45** — Break time. Marco sits in the break area, takes out his phone, looks at
the MTR map. He appears calm. He does not interact with other workers.

**10:00** — Marco returns to work. Mrs. Chan asks him to bag rolls again. He begins
without prompting.

**10:15** — Mixer starts again. Marco reaches for his ear defenders before covering
his ears. He puts them on without being reminded. He continues working.

**10:30** — Marco finishes his second tray. He turns to Mrs. Chan and says, without
prompting: "What should I do next?" Mrs. Chan asks him to help with packing.

**10:45** — Session ends. K. Wong debriefs with Marco. Marco says: "The mixers were
loud but I had my ear defenders. I asked Mrs. Chan two questions. I knew the answer
to the third one."
