## Transcript (simulated)

**11:00** — Priya arrives at Room 1A (class moved from 3B this term). She sits at the
edge of the group table, takes out her sketchbook and begins drawing (regulation).

**11:03** — Ms. Liu greets the class, reads the role list for the poster project: "Priya,
you are the colour scout." Priya writes the role in her notebook twice, without speaking.

**11:08** — Group work starts: agree a poster concept for the school open day. Three
students propose ideas quickly. Priya draws a small palette and two poster sketches on
her own sheet.

at her sheet for a few seconds, then reads out: "Peel palette base with ochre accents —
the gradient picks the texture."

the group continues. She looks across at the table top but does not join the discussion.

her palette.

bring the concept to the group at 11:38?" Priya reads it, nods, continues.

**11:38** — Group stalls on "single paper or two sheets?" Priya offers, unprompted:
"The smooth paper would take the gradient better for the texture." The group agrees.

**11:40** — Session ends. Ms. Liu thanks everyone. Priya packs, keeps sketching as she
leaves. K. Wong debriefs her on the corridor: she says "It helped that I had my role.
I knew what I could say."
